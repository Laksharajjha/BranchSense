# Code of Conduct

BranchSense is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically focused
community. All contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks or harassment.
- Welcome different backgrounds, perspectives, and levels of experience.
- Keep project spaces focused on constructive technical collaboration.
- Respect privacy and do not publish confidential information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, targeted
insults, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption of project discussions.

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer through the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Laksharajjha). Do not use a
public issue for sensitive reports. Reports will be handled confidentially and
reviewed fairly.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
